# Reference Folder — Index

Research and influences for *Standing Water*. This folder is **input, not authority**.
Nothing here overrides `craft/lessons-learned.md`, and nothing here is a licence to
show off. Its whole purpose is to make the mundane half of the credit arrangement in
`craft/craft-principles.md` §1 cheap and accurate, so the impossible half can be paid
for.

## How to use it

- **Before drafting a period story**, skim `period/timeline-1850-1920.md` and the one
  or two topic files the story actually touches. Do not read the whole folder.
- **Take objects, not adjectives.** The right noun — a *hay knife*, a *moss pole*, a
  *crape veil pinned at the temple* — buys more period than any amount of diction.
  See `style-guide.md` → Diction.
- **Two authentic terms per page, maximum.** `period/anachronism-traps.md` is a
  negative checklist, not a vocabulary list to spend from.
- **Cite nothing in the prose.** No character explains history to another character.

### influences.md (Mark's, at the top level)
**Mark's own statement of what the book is reaching for**, and the highest authority
in this folder. Where it and anything in `influences/` disagree, it wins — same rule
as `craft/00-INDEX.md`. It also names influences the annotated files below do not
cover, because they are in copyright and could not be downloaded: Aickman, Jackson,
Sarah Waters, Susan Hill, Elizabeth Hand, Kelly Link, O'Connor, Welty, McCarthy.

`texts/weird-pulp/` as reading only.
