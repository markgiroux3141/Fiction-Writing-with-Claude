Photographed sheets used as the page ground of the facsimile.

`build.sh` prefers this directory over `cover art/blank pages/`. Drop PNG or JPEG
sheets in here and they are picked up in filename order, so page N is stable across
builds.

See **The ground: photographed paper** in `render/README.md` for what to generate:
aspect must match the trim, ~2000x3000, about sixteen of them, and no torn edges —
they are cropped off by design.
