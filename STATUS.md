## What round four decided, and what it declined

**The religious layer is half in.** The review's design was that *the monster is a
rule-follower and so is the Church*, cashed by planting the priest's extended-arms posture
once and unremarked so the reader silently joins it to the laid-out child's arms and to the
game. **That plant is declined**, and the argument is now doctrine: the join would have
been *completed* rather than searched, so it passes L-006's good-gap test — but what the
reader completes is a **meaning**, and with the posture planted, *what was it* acquires a
one-line answer. That is D-004's only real test. It is also a fourth recurrence of the
story's one image, which Mark declined a round earlier on exactly these grounds.

→ **D-004 gains: a rhyme is a cost if it attaches to a character and an explanation if it
attaches to the thing.** Attach it to a person and it prices the haunting; attach it to the
haunting and it explains it. `craft-principles.md` §4's *"a thing that is the shape of grief
is an explanation"* generalises — substitute devotion.

**What went in instead** is the half that attaches to Alma: a grace said at the noon table
by a child who goes too fast and is made to do it again, never appearing again across three
burials and a dying child's first food in nine days; a priest built to be the doctor — late,
a boy at eleven, a plot and a fee in a ledger, *"He said what he says"*, gone; four words of
unbaptised pressure in the mother's letter; and the load-bearing clause of the whole layer,
eight words long — *he had not seen Hollis at Mass since the spring and said so to him and
not to her, the way you would mention a fence.*

**That clause is what makes the layer affordable.** The draft names eight Sundays and the
final scene is one of them; Hollis is home *because* it is a Sunday, which is L-019's
per-character fix and cannot move. A priest in that world lands on the review's own hardest
limit — *why does nobody send for him* — and the answer, never stated and never raised, is
that he was never a person this house sent for. The same clause makes her isolation
structural instead of temperamental and her defeat in the miasma argument more plausible.

**Also declined:** the prohibition (a priest cautioning her against practices at trees — it
gives her final walk a category, it is a planted-and-fulfilled structure, and it gives the
tall one folklore, which is provenance) and the fourth voice as *"a completely incompatible
direction"* (every available line either states the story's epistemology in a character's
mouth or categorises Alma in a way that hands the ending a folk-practice reading).

**Two faults the review did not find**, both mine and both in draft 03 since round three:
a flat continuity contradiction — a letter *"put in the press"* two sentences from *"the
press had been shut and Alma did not open it"* — and a checkable arithmetic slip, *eight
nights* between a Monday and a Friday. And the review's own unlocated third thesis
statement, which it guessed was in the Hollis-and-Ada material: it is the wash-bench
sentence joining cause to cost, which is L-006's one prohibition.

serve no plot function* — and round four is the first draft to pass it. Cobb the hand,
named once and never seen; the mule's feet; the Bertrand gin sold for less than they gave.

## The doctrine change of 2026-09-09 — rules have a stage

**Mark, reading `bible/plate-explorations/attitude-of-grief.md`:** *"We might be crippling
ourselves before we even start the race with these... I see a lot of ideas that could be good
being scrapped because of incredibly strict rules."*

He is right, and the diagnosis is **L-022**. Every prohibition and every test in this repo was
extracted from critique of **finished prose**. I had been applying them as eligibility filters
on **ideas** — the one stage where supply is free and variance is the entire product. The
sharpest case: I ruled a premise out on D-004, whose test is *could a reader finish this and
answer "what was it" in a line* — a test on a finished story. A premise is a one-line answer
by construction, so run at invention it forbids everything in the book.

**The rule adds a stage index above the beat index:** invent takes no rules at all; select may
**flag** repetition and never kill on it, and the pick is Mark's; draft turns the prohibitions
on; revise runs everything. Deferral is free rather than a trade, because the prohibitions
describe sentences and at invention there are none.

**What was restored the same day:** the seed pool's eighteen-item modal band (listed, not
burned); the whole wear-and-pressure family on the angel plate, which I had killed off one
150-word vignette **that has never been scored** — evidence for the ban: zero; the
sculptor's-model off-page rule; and the three banned reader-guesses. `invention-protocol.md`
Part 3 is rewritten from a blacklist into a **repetition register**, its step 0 reordered so
the rule off-page and the four tests come *after* selection, and `ai-tells.md` §F gained a
stage note.

**L-022 narrows rather than adds**, which is the only kind of doctrine STATUS says is worth
writing: it puts a stage precondition on every other rule in the file.

**And a second change the same day, from the same file: L-023 — propose the scare, not the
specification.** Mark: *"lots of minutiae about type of screws... but not a lot about the
things that's scary. When I look at an image, I try to get my mind to a place that sends a
shiver down my spine and form an idea based on that."* A candidate is now a **rendered
moment** — 60-140 words in which something happens — tested by reading it cold and asking
whether anything happens to you. The example he objected to was *the panel is refastened with
the wrong screws*: good evidence inside a finished story, worthless as a proposal, because it
only works on a reader already inside a world that does not exist yet.

**The uncomfortable part: the project already had the right artefact and I substituted lists.**
`vignette-bank.md` exists for exactly this and `CLAUDE.md` says test on vignettes before
stories. `bible/plate-explorations/attitude-of-grief-beats.md` is the first file written to
the format — twelve beats on the angel plate, contradicting each other on purpose, awaiting
*which ones did something to you.* Machinery is now derived after a beat lands, never proposed
instead of one.
