# Story 01 — retrospective

Written 2026-09-09, on promotion. **4,567 words, eleven critique rounds, one draft
file.** The first story in the project, and the first time any of the doctrine ran at
story length.

The individual rules are in `craft/lessons-learned.md`. This file records the patterns
**across** the round, which no single rule captures and which are the reason to keep the
critique log at all.

---

## 1. Every critique was structural or logical. None was about prose.

Eleven rounds and not one note about a sentence being badly made. The notes were:
an event that was over-systematised, an ending pool with one idea in it, an event
under-rendered, a paragraph of proof, an orphaned pronoun, a protagonist behaving
implausibly, an inert scene, a duration that measured instead of frightening, a costume
made too neatly, a three-day delay, and a schedule that domesticated the horror.

**The craft folder is roughly 90% prose-and-mood and it was almost never the thing that
needed fixing.** `style-guide.md` did not come up once. `ai-tells.md` §A caught nothing,
because the banned phrases were never going to appear. What failed was **story logic**,
and the docs had very little to say about it until this round.

Consequence: `ai-tells.md` §D gained *the uncannily calm protagonist*, §6 gained the
fight precondition, and L-019 exists at all.

## 2. Three failures were correct rules fired at the wrong beat.

Flatness used mid-story (→ L-018). Accommodation used before the fight (→ L-019).
Duration used on a fear the reader could already see (→ L-006, third amendment).

None of these were misreadings. Each was the right instrument in the wrong place, and
the reason is now clear and slightly embarrassing: **most of the doctrine was extracted
from a 200-word vignette that consisted entirely of aftermath.** Flatness, accommodation,
duration and permanence are all *last-movement* instruments. In a paragraph, the last
movement is the whole paragraph. At 4,500 words it is the last tenth, and reaching for
those tools earlier hollows out the middle.

**Harvested as the beat index** at the top of `lessons-learned.md`. That table is the
most transferable thing to come out of this story.

## 3. The failure mode is making things add up.

Every critique cut elaboration. **Not one asked for more of anything** — the closest was
*"more emphasis on her mental state"*, and satisfying it meant deleting a duration figure
and a weather list to make room.

| Round | Cut | The instinct underneath |
|---|---|---|
| Event pool | categories D, E, F, G — 12 candidates | mechanism |
| Ending pool | all 18 candidates | one move in eighteen costumes |
| Movement 2 | the alibi survey | proof |
| Movement 2 | — | (a cut that orphaned a pronoun) |
| Movement 3 | the calm wash-bench scene | plot convenience |
| Ending r2 | eleven years, the weather list | arithmetic |
| Ending r4 | "every day at two" | a schedule, i.e. an accommodation |

Mechanism, proof, arithmetic, schedules: one instinct wearing four hats. **Coherence is
not a horror value.** Logged as a standing tendency in L-018 and as L-016.

## 4. The rule-breaking beat needed no correction. The rule-following beats needed nine.

DEV-003 broke L-017 deliberately — dramatising the chase where the rule said summarise.
It survived four further passes untouched. Everything derived *from* the doctrine —
the elegant oblique last line, the duration, the accumulation, the schedule — was
corrected.

This is L-014 replicating at story scale: **rules preserve discoveries; they do not
produce them.** Treat every prescription as a floor.

## 5. Rules replicated, which is new.

Before this story almost every rule in the repo was `Basis: 1`. This round produced the
first independent replications:

| Rule | Basis | What replicated |
|---|---|---|
| **L-001** | 2 → **4** | behaviour-only under-rendering, twice more; then *discovered, not felt* |
| **L-003** | 1 → **2** | a referent the reader could not name — resolved to the wrong noun |
| **L-017** | 1 → **2** | say it plainly; fear not pity |
| **L-006** | 1 | **three amendments** — the most-corrected rule in the repo |

Every replication was a rule I had read, written down myself, and then broken anyway.
**Reading the doctrine is not the same as applying it**, which is the argument for the
pre-flight checklist being run rather than cited.

## 5b. The same model in a fresh context found things the writing context could not.

Added 2026-09-09. `review-of-draft1.txt` was produced by **the same model**, in a clean
context, given the story and none of the craft folder. It found the Emmy flinch, two
thesis lines, the visible architecture and a setting inconsistency — none of which the
writing context had surfaced across eleven rounds.

**Not a capability gap. A context gap**, and the four causes are worth keeping:
commitment to beats I had chosen and defended; eleven rounds of accumulated
justification; the opposite objective (*make this work* versus *what is wrong with
this*); and, most importantly, **doctrine contamination** — my context held the rules, so
I read the story as compliance, while a reader given only prose saw the symmetry for
what it was and named L-014's rubric tell without knowing a rubric existed.

**The sharpest evidence that self-knowledge does not transfer:** sections 1 and 3 of this
very file say *every critique was structural* and *the failure mode is making things add
up* — and I still could not apply either to the draft in front of me.

Formalised as **L-020** / `revision-protocol.md` step 1b: fresh-context review sits
between Draft and Critique, holds nothing but the story, and its job is to stop Mark's
rounds being spent on what a clean read catches. Four of seven notes were actionable.
Useful, not an oracle.

## 6. What worked and should be repeated

- **Movement-by-movement handover.** Roughly 800–1,200 words at a time, critiqued before
  the next was written. Two whole movements were rewritten at a cost of one message each.
  A completed 4,500-word draft would have hidden the same faults behind sunk cost.
- **Mark writing the ending himself.** Eighteen candidates lost to four sentences he
  typed. L-013 is right that the model cannot select, and this round suggests it
  sometimes cannot generate either.
- **The world closing the character's options.** 1878 medicine, a husband's reasonable
  kindness, a sister with room. No supernatural foreclosure anywhere, which kept the
  thing ruleless.
- **Refusing modern interior vocabulary.** *Traumatised* and *catatonic* were both
  offered and both declined; the substitutes — a man in the door of his own kitchen
  waiting to be told, one eye through a badly cut hole — are better than the words would
  have been.

## 7. Open, for story 02

- **The prose docs are untested at length.** `style-guide.md` never came up. Either it is
  right, or nobody has looked.
- **`Physical: threatened`.** Nothing explicit was shown; D-003 wants roughly a third of
  the book explicit, so story 02 or 03 owes that.
- **Ending shape is spent.** Recontextualization used here; L-014 rations shapes to one
  in three, and the accommodation shape is already over-fired. Story 02 should probably
  be *the door left open*.
- **The count.** *Six in the field, five at the table* is spent and so is the tag frame.
- **Untested still:** dialogue at length (this story has very little), first person, and
  a modern setting (D-002).

# Round three — added on the second promotion, 2026-09-09

Draft 01 was promoted and reopened within the hour. Two rounds followed and **draft 03 is
final at 5,119 words.** Round-by-round feedback is `critique-03.md`; what follows is only
the patterns that span it, which no single rule holds.

## 8. Self-knowledge does not transfer across *minutes*, not just across contexts

§5b of this file already recorded that a fresh context saw things the writing context
could not, and named doctrine contamination as the cause. Round three is worse than that
finding.

**In one session, holding this file, having quoted it, I made the same error three times
and Mark corrected it three times:**

| I proposed | Because | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| Alma taught Emmy the rule | it made her complicit cheaply | the kids came up with it themselves; no explanation needed |
| cut *"There is no stone for Emmy Ledoux"* | a recovered body implies a grave | it opens the possibility nobody ever buried her |
| give Hollis a failed attempt on the tree | L-019 says the accommodation must be bought | he is not deciding, he is stopping |

Three different rules, one instinct: **make it add up.** §1 and §3 of this file say so in
those words, and I had them in context each time. The conclusion is narrower and more
useful than §5b's: it is not that a writing context cannot see its own pattern. It is that
**recognising a pattern in one's past output does not fire when the pattern is currently
executing.** The only mechanisms that catch it are external — Mark, or a clean read.

## 9. Every doctrinal change this round narrowed the rulebook

Five additions, zero new prescriptions:

- **L-021** — a prohibition, and it deletes a default nobody had chosen (keep her likeable).
- **D-005** — releases an entire research programme and rules the wider Tier-3b note out.
- **L-016 → basis 3** — two new prohibitive clauses (no origin, no tracked consequences).
- **L-019** — a *limiting* clause: the fight precondition is spent once per character.
- **D-004** — a clarifying clause that permits something previously ambiguous.

STATUS.md's honesty section says a rule that shrinks the rulebook is the work and a rule
invented to feel thorough is the trap. This round was five for five on the first kind,
which is the first time that has been true.

## 10. Two rules wanted the same sentence, and the prohibition won

*"Alma had taught her the rule herself"* was written to satisfy **L-021** (implicate her)
and it violated **L-016** (the game has no provenance). First documented collision in the
repo, and the resolution generalises: **a prohibition does not weaken because a different
lesson is shopping.** Take the effect from elsewhere — the complicity came from the boots,
the pot, the wagon and the letters instead, all of which were already available in the
36-candidate pool and none of which touches the haunting's furniture.

The failure mode to watch: a prohibition breached *in service* of another rule does not
feel like rule-breaking. It feels like diligence.

## 11. Mark's endings have now beaten every pool twice, and the second was constructional

Round one: eighteen candidates lost to four sentences. Round three: I produced an accurate
cost analysis of his tree ending — the refrain, the *no stone* line, the unterminated
afternoon, the POV — and he then constructed the version that paid none of those costs, by
noticing that *"it was open, and it was not on him"* could become a **direction** rather
than a closed door. The generative gap is basis 2 and is not about prose.

**And the round's single most valuable instruction was about form.** On the smell: *"The
repetition of the way it was said should be the linkage, not a direct reference to the
hallway which seems too forced."* That is a craft rule, not a reaction — the first Mark has
stated as such — and it corrected my execution of his own idea. Candidate for promotion
into L-017 if it replicates.

## 12. The pre-flight caught mechanics and nothing else, again

Caught unaided across two drafts: two orphaned referents of my own making (one of them the
*"Neither of them touched the arms"* compression, which is L-003/L-005 replicating for a
third time), a changelog that claimed a clause the page did not carry, and §E item 9 on
Hollis — which I detected and was going to **report** rather than solve, until Mark's
smell solved it.

Structural or judgment faults caught unaided: **none, in either draft.**

The checklist is worth running and it is not a substitute for a reader. Its measured yield
is bookkeeping.

## 13. Passage handover worked a second time

`revisions-03.md` handed over three passages — about 700 words — instead of a 5,000-word
draft. Approved with two corrections, both of which cost one message. Repeat it: hand over
what changed, never the whole thing, and give the reader **computed line ranges** rather
than a paragraph of description (`revision-protocol.md` step 4, added this round at Mark's
request).
