# Story 01 — The game

## Origin

Growth seed chosen by Mark, 2026-09-08 (the L-015 appetite signal, which had been
blocking in `STATUS.md`). Seed text: `craft/vignettes/batch-01-r6.md` §4, *The game*
(vignette I1), r6, unscored.

**Seed event:** a stranger joins a children's game and does not stop — logged `spent`
in `invention-protocol.md` Part 3. Spent guards against *other* stories reusing it; this
story is the promotion of it, which is the whole point of L-015.

## Rule (off-page) — L-008

> **There is something out there with the children, and the ones it is near sicken.**

One sentence, and deliberately thin. **It has no rules beyond this** (L-016): no rule
set, no schedule, no arithmetic, no score. Mark, 2026-09-08: *"the whole point is just
that there is a strange entity out there with them, that we don't know until she
counts."* The earlier vignette-scale rule — *it learns a game by watching and plays it
correctly, and will come in if it is called* — is **withdrawn**. It was a mechanism, and
"comes in if called" in particular implies a rule the reader could work out and act on.

Never on the page in any form, including implied. The test: could a reader finish this
and answer *what was it* in a line? If yes, I put a mechanism in.

**Contact is permitted, and this was a live worry until Mark's exemplar settled it.**
A1 has them sicken *in the order it caught them*, which links being touched to being
ill — and I was treating that as an L-016 risk. It is not. The exemplar has a creature
take a boy by the arm and the boy sicken, with no account of why. **One fact of contact
is not a mechanism; rules about contact are.** They are playing tag. It touches them.
That is all the reader gets and all the reader needs.

## Wound (D-004) — what it costs, not what it means

Her children sicken one by one and at least one is laid out in the house, and nothing
accounts for it. The cost is paid in nursing, in shifts, and in the fact that she saw
something and counted, and the counting is all she will ever have.

**Not a thesis.** Not about grief, inheritance, exclusion, or arithmetic-as-power. If
this reads as *the story is about X*, it is wrong and gets rewritten.

## The event — chosen, not arrived at (L-012)

Mark selected **A1 + B4 + C3** from the 24-candidate pool. Rejected D, E, F, G outright.
Full record and quotes in `event-options.md`.

**The spine, in his words:**

> *"the woman notices a tall figure with the children, when they come in she notices it
> wasn't with the children, they get sick one by one, and it can even end mysteriously."*

- **The game is tag.** Not an invented game. No rules named, no correctness, no score.
  Children's games are nonsense to adults and that is the asset, not a problem to fix.
- **The count is one line, not a subplot.** Six in the field, five at the table. One of
  them wasn't real. It happens once and is never reconciled, repeated, or written down.
- **B4** — it is gone when she looks for it. Mark liked this specifically.
- **C3** — the neighbour woman lays out the body and asks, without much interest, why
  this family lays its dead out with the arms straight out to the sides.

### The §11 child-harm limit

Treated as **satisfied, not overruled.** §11 permits child harm where the harm is what
the story is actually about; under this spine the illness is the story rather than a
decoration on it. Flagged to Mark; he chose A1 and C3 with the flag in front of him.

## The particulars — fixed 2026-09-08

**Not an outline.** L-015 bars one: *"an outline is where collapse happens first."* These
are the concrete facts the prose is built out of — §1's boring reality, deposited so the
impossible can be withdrawn. Shape is not decided here and will be written last if at
all.

**When.** July into August, **1878**. The lower-Mississippi epidemic year, so a household
losing children is ordinary and the district has an explanation ready that costs it
nothing. Germ theory is not general; nobody present has a framework. Pre-1880s parish
means a carpenter makes the box and a neighbour woman lays out the body — which C3
requires.

**Where.** A cane farm in an invented Gulf-South parish. D-001: no shared geography with
any other story. A gallery, a yard, a fig tree in the middle of it, a cistern, a ditch,
the cane starting where the yard stops.

**The household.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Alma** | the woman on the gallery | POV, close third, the whole story |
| **Hollis** | her husband | down in the cane before light, up at dinner. *"Not a man who played."* |
| **Mattie** | 12, hers | too old for the game, plays anyway, badly on purpose |
| **Sam** | 10, hers | the one who runs out into the sun |
| **Wick** | 8, hers | |
| **Nan** | 6, hers | gets caught constantly and does not mind |
| **the Ledoux girl** | 9, **not hers** | *Emmy.* A neighbour's child, in the house for the summer |

Five children in the yard, four of them Alma's. **The one she has to answer for is the
one who goes behind the tree.** That is the residue and it needs no explanation: three
graves she can show a mother, and for the Ledoux girl nothing at all.

**What happens to whom — and the pattern is that there isn't one.**

- **Nan**, first. Rendered on the page.
- **Mattie**, second. Compressed.
- **Wick**, third. **C3 lands here** — the neighbour woman has laid out two already, which
  is the only way *"why does this family lay its dead out with the arms out"* can be
  asked.
- **Sam** never sickens at all. Nobody ever knows why. He is the one who has to go on
  living in that house.
- **Emmy Ledoux**, last, and she does not die. She goes behind the tree.

Three dead, one vanished, one untouched, same summer, same thing. **No rule to extract**
— this is the L-016 guard, and it is load-bearing. Specifically: the two children under
sheets in the opening must **not** be the ones who die. Any correspondence I notice
myself building gets broken on purpose.

**The banked image (L-017): the full sun.** It stands in it, arms down, and does not
mind, while the children keep inside the ring of the fig's shade. Planted in movement
one as weather, never touched again, paid in the last sentence after the yard is empty.
A second image is banked in the same scene — **the arms down at the sides** — and that
one is paid mid-story at C3, not at the close. Two banked images, two different payoffs,
no collision in the final beat.

**The approach signal (L-010), absence not presence.** The yard is dry enough that the
children raise dust to their knees. It does not. One line, unremarked, never explained.

**The illness is never named by anyone with authority.** Alma sees what a mother sees: a
child off her food, a throat, a smell, a slowness. The district says fever. A modern
reader can half-explain the sickness from the cistern in the yard and **still** has
nothing for the figure or the tree — which is D-004 working as intended. Do not press
this; it is furniture, not a clue.

  candidates.** The last child, near death, leaps from the bed and runs out; the figure
  reappears; they go round the fig tree playing; she goes behind the trunk and is not
  seen again. Full record, argument and risk in `ending-options.md`. Execution deviates
  from L-017 and is logged as **DEV-003**: the chase is the penultimate beat, the last
  beat is cold and after the fact.
  **Two things I must hold while drafting.** (1) The other four died and stayed dead —
  same summer, same thing, different outcomes, therefore no extractable rule. Nobody
  treats the vanishing as the game's outcome and the earlier deaths do not fall into a
  pattern. (2) The tree must be narrow, in full daylight, with a clear view all the way
  round; the impossibility is the width of the trunk, not an effect.
- **Open: which image the last line cashes.** `ending-options.md` is now a menu of
  banked images rather than of endings. One must be laid down plainly in the first third
  and paid off in the final flat sentence.

  **Rules out** the L-006 accommodation ending, which is over-fired anyway (nine of

## What the doctrine learned from this round

Recorded here because it was expensive and the loop is the point of the project.
Twenty-four candidates, and Mark rejected half of them for a fault none of my four tests
could see: they had **mechanisms**. L-012's *implies more than itself* passed every one
of them. Logged as **L-016**, with boundaries added to L-012 and L-013, and
`craft-principles.md` §3 and §4 amended.
