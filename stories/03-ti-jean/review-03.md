## Applied

1. **Cut `which is how anything in that parish gets said`** (M1). A gloss and an appositive,
   flagged by both.
2. **Cut `Nothing changed, because nothing had been said`** (M3). **This reverses my own
   decline in review-02's triage**, where I kept it as load-bearing for L-021. Two things
   settle it against me: review 03's argument, which I had no answer to — *"the Ovide
   paragraph does the same work dramatically, and it's better than any of the glosses because
   it makes the reader do it"* — and my own inconsistency, since I cut *"She noticed that and
   did nothing with it"* as authorial grading in the same pass and kept this, which is the
   same move. The scene already carries it: she comes at it sideways, Azélie agrees with all
   of it and goes further, and Elmire irons two aprons, drinks a cup of coffee she does not
   want, and goes home. The paragraph now ends there.
3. **`the way anything in that parish gets come at, which is by talking about something else
   first`** → *by talking about something else first for as long as it took.*
4. **`Two crosses of any size`** → *There were only two crosses of any size in that ground.*

## Escalated to Mark — the three that matter

### 1. The register never modulates. This is the best note either review produced.

> Every sentence is flat, declarative, withheld. By section 4 the reader has fully adapted,
> and suppression you can't feel isn't suppression — it's just the prose style. The one place
> pressure escapes is *"She was angry. She turned it over the whole way home and could not
> find one person to be angry at,"* and it's two sentences long, and it's the best emotional
> moment in the piece. A story about people who can't say the sentence needs at least one
> moment where the seam splits. Right now the most controlled paragraph in the story (Section
> 4, *"Her arm kept going"*) is asking to be the loudest, and it's pitched identically to the
> coping maintenance in paragraph one.

This is `ai-tells.md` §B's rhythmic uniformity operating at **paragraph and movement scale**
rather than sentence scale, and the repo has no rule covering it: every prohibition we hold
pushes toward flatness (L-006, L-017, §11's evenest sentence) and **nothing in the doctrine
says the flat register needs something to be flat against.** The story has one dynamic level,
so its climax cannot be louder than its opening.

Not actioned: where the seam splits, and how far, is a compositional decision about the
story's centre. My recommendation is that it splits **once**, in M4, between seeing him and
turning round — and that it splits in **syntax**, not in vocabulary. No named emotion, no new
adjective; one sentence that runs long or breaks where every other sentence in the story
stops cleanly. Everything before and after stays exactly as flat as it is.

### 2. `He had never liked being looked at` has no floor under it — and the floor is a beat I held back.

> It's the last characterizing fact we get about Ti-Jean, and nothing earlier supports it — in
> fact Father Ancelet looking straight at him is staged as the decent thing, and Ti-Jean is
> otherwise entirely unbothered by attention. If the line is meant to be the parish inventing
> a reason, one small earlier beat would let a reader see it as invention rather than as
> revelation.

Correct, and the missing beat is identified in `exploration-audit.md` under *flagged, not
decided*: **prose-04 §1** — Ovide says good morning to Ti-Jean's hands and goes on looking at
them until it is over, and then Ti-Jean says good morning to Elmire and **she does the same
thing and cannot finish her sentence looking up.** I recommended holding it because Mark had
flagged the audit risk on the hands tell himself. A cold reader has now reported the cost of
holding it, from the other end: without it, **F7 has nothing to be an exception to.** Father
Ancelet looking straight at him is only decent against a parish that doesn't.

That is L-024 — *the exception needs the rule already faintly in place* — arriving on the
**gaze** axis instead of the avoidance axis. Same rule, same failure, third time in this
story. My recommendation: take prose-04 §1, in M1 or M2, once, and let the aunt's line become
visibly the parish's invention rather than a fact about him.

### 3. The dream. Both reviews say it fails; they prescribe opposite cures.

- review-02: *"If that gladness were in her hand while her arm kept painting the coping,
  section 4 would be unbearable. As written the dream is a mood insert."*
- review-03: *"Cut the dream, or cut everything in it except waking up with her arm out and
  lying to Alcide about a dog. Let the comfort arrive cold."* And the sharp part: *"'In the
  dream the gladness was her own' pre-explains the thing that should detonate in section 5.
  When we get to the hat on the plinth and 'That he had been comfortable,' we've already been
  told."*

**My recommendation is the synthesis, and it is small: keep the dream, cut the gloss on it.**
Remove *"That was the part she could not get round afterward. In the dream the gladness was
her own."* — two sentences. *She was glad.* standing on its own line is the whole of it, and
Mark's own note on this beat says exactly that: *the gladness is the whole of it.* By that
standard the sentence explaining that the gladness matters is surplus, and it is what spends
*he had been comfortable* in advance. Review-02's cut list had these same two sentences and I
declined them; this is the second blind read to arrive at them.

Cutting the dream entire is refused: it is Mark's own invention, and the gladness is the only
place in the story where something in her wants what the thing wants — which is the
complicity both reviews say the story is short of.

## Declined, with the argument

**Cutting `He said it was watching at her`.** Review 03: *"two uncanny-child lines in adjacent
paragraphs… The second is much better. Cut the first."* Refused on three grounds:

1. **review-02 named that sentence as one of three it would have noticed missing** — *"a
   nine-year-old's preposition doing more than the previous 1,200 words. This is the only
   place in the story where the impossible thing gets a grammar."* The two cold readers rate
   this line at opposite extremes, which means it is doing something, not nothing.
2. **The correction generates the question.** *Watching at her* is what makes *could she see
   it back* inevitable; without it the second line is a non-sequitur a child produced.
3. **It was fragmented once already and Mark had it put back** (L-025, 2026-09-10). Cutting
   the first half is the same fragmentation from the other end.

The underlying observation is fair and has a cheaper fix: **Numa exists to deliver two lines
and be sent round by the road.** One beat of him doing something that serves nothing would
retire the charge.

**Speeding up the opening.** *"The opening three paragraphs are slow — the coping history is
good texture but the figure is the hook and it's paragraph four."* Held, and noted as being in
tension with this review's own closing paragraph, which says the maintenance is the story's
one original thing and asks for **more** of it. The figure arriving fourth is §1 — the mundane
buys the impossible — and L-009 requires the frame first. If Mark wants the opening tightened
it should come out of the middle of M1, not off the front.

## The note to keep, and it is a collection-level one

> The real originality here isn't the statue — a hostile thing a community manages by refusing
> to name it is well-trodden ground, Jackson especially. What's yours is the maintenance. The
> whitewash twice a year, too much sand in the mortar in 1890-whenever, lime cracking on her
> wrists, the mortar going off in the pan and putting her half an hour behind. The horror is
> administered by women with brushes, on a schedule, forever, and Elmire ends up keeping the
> plot of the boy she didn't warn. **Push further into the labor. That's the part nobody else
> has.**

Two things follow. First, it is the one direction in this review that asks for *addition*
rather than subtraction, and it points at the material the story already does best — the same
place D-005 says specification earns its keep by doing work in the scene. Second, it names the
neighbour the premise sits next to (Jackson), which belongs in `book-bible.md` rather than here.
