# Audit of `bible/plate-explorations/` against the draft

Run 2026-09-10 on Mark's question: *"is there any other prose in the files in plate-explorations
that deserves a home in any of the movements we have created?"* Every prose file read in full —
`-prose-01` through `-prose-04`, `-variants`, `-beats`, `-beats-02` — not grepped. This file
exists so the question never has to be asked twice, and so no scored beat dies silently again
(L-023 downstream clause, L-025).

superseded table bars reuse.

## Held for M5, which is not written yet

- **"He was with somebody all morning. He was with everybody all morning."** (`-prose-03`.)
  Available in M5 in past perfect; unavailable in M4, where she does not yet know. Restatement
  that *intensifies*, which L-005 permits.
- **The shears habit** (`-prose-01`): she holds them the whole time she is in that ground —
  walking, standing, talking — puts them in the basket only outside the gate, and never cuts
  anything with them. **An object carrying permanence instead of a number**, which is L-006's own
  stated preference over a tally. Strong M5 price candidate.
- **D5's frost** — the coat folded on the plinth under the hat is not one of Mark's two picks
  (D1 + D9) and stays out, but *frost on the flour barrels at first light and the morning not
  warming until ten* is free and belongs to nothing else.
