## The review, verbatim

> What fired on the first pass, without effort: Ovide going round by the road after a
> lifetime of cutting through. Elmire walking fast through one stretch and refusing to call
> it a decision. Somebody taking Ti-Jean off it every single time, never the same person
> twice. Those three are safe. A first-time reader gets them.
>
> What I only saw on the second pass, hunting: the shell. Packed hard as far as the third
> row, loose past that, stated twice. That's a footprint census. It tells you exactly how
> many people go up the top of that path and how often, and it's the hardest evidence in the
> story because it can't be a mood. I read it as terrain the first time. I'd guess most
> readers do. Same with the north face in the mornings, which you've given a perfectly good
> reason for, and the lamb earning its keep because it's the only thing a child asks to see,
> which implies something about what children don't ask to see.
>
> So the avoidance lands. It just lands mostly on the three explicit human behaviors and not
> on the physical evidence, which means it reads as personal squeamishness rather than as a
> fact about the ground.
>
> The contrast is the softer thing, and I don't think it's a subtlety problem. It's a
> distance problem. Ti-Jean going toward it is in section 1. Everyone going around it is in
> section 2. The reader holds two separate facts rather than one figure against its ground.
> In section 1 you don't yet know that his walking up the path is unusual, because you
> haven't been told anyone declines to. By section 2 he's offstage.
>
> There's also a complication in your own material that may be blurring it. Elmire isn't an
> avoider. Her plot is in the fifth row, so she has to go past. She's the one person in the
> parish with a standing obligation to walk through the bad stretch, twice a year plus
> whenever else, and she's built a whole practice around doing it without admitting she's
> doing anything. That's more interesting than avoidance, and it means the clean opposition
> isn't Ti-Jean against everyone. It's Ti-Jean going toward, most people going around, and
> Elmire going through with her head turned.
>
> If you want the contrast to register without stating it, the cheapest fix is adjacency
> rather than addition. When he breaks for the figure in section 1, the surrounding women are
> already established as working their rows. You could let one physical fact of the general
> avoidance sit in that same paragraph, so his motion has something to be measured against at
> the moment it happens. The shell going loose under his feet past the third row would do it,
> since you've already planted the compaction two paragraphs earlier and it costs you one
> clause.
>
> I wouldn't say more than that. The restraint is the story's whole method, and the reader
> who works for the arithmetic is going to be a much better reader of the last section than
> one who was handed it.

## Triage

**1. TAKEN — the adjacency fix.** Three edits, twelve words net:
- M1's ground paragraph now places her: *the rows ran off it on both sides and hers was the
  fifth on the north side*. Without it the loose shell in M1 is bare terrain, because the
  reader does not yet know she works above the turn.
- M1's break paragraph gains one flat sentence between his motion and her hand: **"The shell
  went over his shoes."** Verbatim echo of the plant three paragraphs earlier, no
  explanation attached, and it also collects the child's shoes from the paragraph before it.
  It buys a beat of him walking, which makes her hand read faster.
- M2 elides the row, which is now a second mention (L-005).

**2. DECLINED — making the footprint census land.** The review's diagnosis is right and its
implied remedy is barred. *"The hardest evidence in the story because it can't be a mood"* is
precisely the objection L-016 basis 4 exists to enforce: hard evidence that the avoidance
**works** converts a feeling in the parish into a property of the object, which is a rule,
which is a mechanism. The shell reading as terrain is the deniability functioning, not
failing — and the boring cause is already on the page (the through-route turns off at the
third row). The review cannot know this, because it holds no standing decisions. Consistent
with L-020's draft-03 calibration: **the declines cluster on the review's most confident
idea.**

**3. DECLINED as an edit, KEPT as a reading — Elmire is not an avoider.** The three-way
structure it names (Ti-Jean toward, most people around, Elmire through with her head turned)
is the story's architecture and it cashes in M4, where she is the one person with an
obligation up there and she turns round and finishes the coping. No edit: it found the design
rather than a fault.

**4. NOT PLANTED, NOT TOUCHED — the lamb.** *"The only thing a child asks to see, which
implies something about what children don't ask to see."* Nobody wrote that; it is what the
sentence does. Leave it alone.

**5. Where it disagrees with itself, and I side with the review's second thought.** It says
*"by section 2 he's offstage"*, which is not quite so: the maman paragraph sits directly under
Ovide going round by the road, which is the juxtaposition it asks for. It does not land
because the paragraph spends its second half dismantling itself — *so it was nothing* — and
that undercut has to come last or it is not an undercut. Left as written.

**6. Process finding: run this per movement, not per draft.** L-020 places the review between
Draft and Critique on a *finished draft*. Run at 1,800 words it cost nothing and found a
structural ordering fault while the fix was still twelve words. At 4,500 the same fault costs
a round. → clause added to L-020.
