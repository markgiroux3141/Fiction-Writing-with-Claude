We are drafting **story 03** of *Standing Water*. It is fully invented and planned; your job
is prose, one movement at a time.

**Read first, in this order:**

1. `stories/03-ti-jean/notes.md` — front matter, the one impossible thing, and **What must not happen**. Read that last section twice.
2. `stories/03-ti-jean/skeleton.md` — five movements, in order, with each movement's job.
3. `stories/03-ti-jean/source-material.md` — **the approved beats, in the canonical register. Draft from this file.** It ends with a table of superseded files; do not draft from anything in that table.
4. `craft/lessons-learned.md` — start at *The status of these rules*, then the **stage index**, then the **beat index**. Prohibitions are near-absolute; prescriptions are optional and never required; the effect on the page outranks all of it.
5. `craft/ai-tells.md` §A, §C, §E, §F.
6. `craft/style-guide.md` and `craft/craft-principles.md` §§1–7.

**What this story is.** 1905, a Gulf South parish. Close third on Elmire, in scene, past tense.
A cemetery figure that nobody can date and that everybody in the parish walks a little faster
past, without ever saying so. Ti-Jean Guidry, nineteen, small and marked in a way the prose
never describes, is the one person unaffected by it — he goes for it every time he is taken out
there, and is always led away by whoever has charge of him, gently, with a hand on the shoulder.
On the Saturday before All Saints' there are forty people in the ground and everybody assumes
he is with somebody. He reaches it and dies there.

**The register, which is the whole job.** Plain, concrete, unhurried. The frightening line is
short, flat, and last, and no adjective does any work in it. **Nobody in the scene reacts** —
the fear is a thing that happens in Elmire's body while she is doing something else, and she
never names it and never admits it. The horror arrives obliquely: in a child's flat correction,
in a habit nobody can account for, in a small true statement about how the world works that the
thing has quietly broken. Fear is never described; it is priced.

**Six things that will ruin it, in order of likelihood:**

1. **Explaining.** The figure never acts, never changes, never responds. No origin, no rules, no dates, no provenance. If a reader can finish this and answer *what was it* in a line, the draft has failed (D-004).
2. **A crowd obeying at once.** The avoidance is one person at a time, deniable, overridable in principle — walking a little faster and not slowing down, because slowing down would be a decision. Twenty people all avoiding the same thing is a force field, and a force field is a mechanism (L-016 basis 4).
3. **Describing Ti-Jean.** No clinical account of his deformity, ever, in any register. It exists only in what the world arranges around it: the hats his aunt makes, the shopkeeper who asks his aunt whether the boy wants a peppermint, where people put their eyes. Do not repeat one tell — a repeated tell gets audited.
4. **Describing the body.** He is never found in the figure's posture and the scene is never shown. The reader gets exactly two things: *they could not get his hand down*, and *the box stayed closed*.
5. **Cruelty.** The family is tired and ordinary and stretched, never mean. A moral structure is something to solve.
6. **Aftermath instruments fired early.** Flatness, accommodation, duration and permanent cost belong in **movement 5 only**. Reaching for them in movements 1–3 hollows out the middle. And no tallies of years used as cadence — check any number against the character's actual age.

**Method.** Write **movement one only**, 800–1,000 words, and stop. Hand it over and wait. Two
whole movements of story 01 were rewritten at a cost of one message each because they were
handed over singly; a finished draft hides the same faults behind sunk cost.

Write to `stories/03-ti-jean/draft-01.md`, appending each movement as it is approved. Never
overwrite a draft — a new round is a new file.

**When the full draft exists**, run the twelve-item pre-flight in `craft/ai-tells.md` §E and
report failures honestly rather than quietly fixing them, then run the fresh-context review
(L-020) before Mark reads it. That review has been skipped before and it cost a whole round.

**Open, and Mark's to close:** the title, and the rule off-page in `notes.md`, which is
provisional and mine rather than his.
