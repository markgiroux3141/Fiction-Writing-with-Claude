**Status: nothing chosen.** No premise, no event, no plate, no period. Created
2026-09-11 on Mark's question: *a ship of smugglers, 19th-century-ish — how does that
tie into how we've been writing, and does it break any rules?*

## 1. Does it break a rule?

**No prohibition, and no standing decision.** Audited against D-001–D-005 and
L-001–L-025. A vessel is not barred anywhere, D-001 is satisfied trivially (a hull is
the most standalone setting available), and D-002 puts c. 1850–1920 dead center — which
happens to be the wet-plate window too.

What it strains is listed in §3. None of it is a prohibition; all of it is the kind of
thing that eats a round if it is discovered in draft.

## 2. What it ties into — better than expected

**The labour finding.** `book-bible.md` § *What the book's own register turned out to be*:
the premise is not the differentiator, the labour is. *"The horror is administered by women
with brushes, on a schedule, forever."* A smuggling vessel is almost nothing but labour and
accounts — the manifest that does not match the hold, tonnage dues, the bar pilot, the
bonded warehouse, shares and lays, who eats first, the pump that has to be manned whether
or not anyone believes in the thing. That is the same instrument stories 01 and 03 won on,
in a material the book has not used. **This is the strongest argument for the setting.**

**Confinement is a genuinely new architecture.** Stories 01 and 03 are community stories,
and in both the ordinary world is what beats the protagonist (L-019). On a vessel the
ordinary world is nine men and a hull and there is nowhere to walk to. That changes the
notice → intrude → implicate ladder (§6) rather than repeating it, and it opens the one
unspent ending type in the ledger.

**Flat water, if it stays inshore.** The book is called *Standing Water*. Blue water
breaks the register outright — wind, horizon, weather, scale. Barataria, the passes, the
bays behind the barrier islands, a lugger with the sails hanging in dead air, do not: that
is standing water with a boat in it. **Keep it inside the islands.**

> **Contested, 2026-09-11.** The four photographs Mark added are mostly open sea, and the
> prose samples in `prose-01.md` were all written inshore on the strength of the paragraph
> above. See §3.1. I still think the register argument is right, and it is an argument, not
> a rule — nothing in the craft folder says where the water has to be.

**The vegetal register has a marine dialect.** §11 says vegetal before visceral, and a
hull is a thing that grows and has to be scraped — fouling, worm in the timbers, what
comes up on the careening beach. `the-encrusted-man` already proves the register works on
a person, and the plate inventory flags it as the book's best candidate for the explicit
physical (D-003).

## 3. What it strains

1. ~~**No plate is a ship.**~~ **OBSOLETE, same day.** Mark added four vessel photographs on
   2026-09-11 and they are catalogued as `plate-inventory.md` § VII: `the-ones-at-the-rail`,
   `the-ship-under-way`, `the-ship-alongside`, `the-empty-deck`. Kept here rather than
   deleted, because the argument it was carrying survives the fact that killed it:
   *a portrait plate would have kept the story on a person and off the vessel as spectacle.*
   That was free when no ship existed. It now has to be won on merit, and the four pictures
   make the spectacle route easy, which is the thing to watch. `the-ship-alongside` is the
   one that does not — it is a maintenance photograph of a hull's port side, and it points at
   the labour register rather than at the sea.

   **A new problem arrives with them.** Three of the four are open sea — swell, foam at the
   stem, no land — and §2 below argues that blue water breaks the book's register and the
   passes and bays do not. The plates and that argument disagree. One of them gives, and
   which one is Mark's call.

2. **The ghost ship is one of the most mode-collapsed premises in the language, and
   `ai-tells.md` §F does not cover it** — §F was harvested from a house-and-swamp setting.
   A vessel needs **its own step-0b reader's-guess ban list** before any candidate is
   generated. The ones a reader arrives holding: the derelict found with the table laid,
   the plague ship, the cargo nobody should have opened, the thing following in the wake,
   the Jonah, the man who was never signed on.

3. **The two most obvious ship hauntings are already flagged spent in this repo.** The
   head-count over by one is J2 in `story-seed-pool.md` (*"the tally-over-by-one version of
   him is spent"*), and the boat that sits lower than it should is J5, flagged against
   batch 03 #3. Both are the first things the setting suggests. Worth knowing before
   generation, not after.

4. **Slavery is the shortest path out of a smuggler's hold, and that is Mark's call.**
   `story-seed-pool.md` § *Two subjects that are Mark's call, not mine*: the region's wound
   is available, the modal treatment (avenging spirit of the wronged dead) is burned
   because it explains the haunting (D-004) and uses the history as atmosphere. A vessel
   running contraband after 1808 points straight at it — more directly than anything else
   in the book. **This needs deciding before candidates are generated**, because it changes
   what the ship is carrying and therefore every event on the list. Three routes:
   (a) a trade that is not that — blockade running 1862–64, specie, mercury, hides, liquor,
   later labour smuggling; (b) the peripheral route `folklore-and-belief.md` §4 prescribes
   for conjure, in the present tense of the ship's own economy — the lay, the shares, the
   account, who is owed; (c) not at all.

5. **A ship is a timetable, and the beat index bans the timetable as haunt-logic.** Watches,
   bells, soundings, the pump, the tide. *"A recurring schedule: never fires. A ritual on a
   timetable is an accommodation and domesticates the horror."* Fine as texture — that is
   the labour. Fatal if the thing attaches to it, and the setting pushes hard that way.

6. **Sailors' lore is a mechanism-delivery system.** L-016 bars rules, origin, score and
   demonstrated consistency; L-008 and D-004 bar explanation. A crew talking superstition
   will explain the haunting inside two pages and it will read as authentic while doing it.

7. **An ensemble produces an explainer.** §8 wants first or close third. A crew invites
   nine speaking parts, and one of them always becomes the man who knows. One man with a
   job, and the rest as weather.

8. **The register risk is the largest single hazard, and it is not a rule at all — it is
   the voice.** The bible bans the romp; the tone is *"nothing in this book is in a hurry,
   and neither is the prose."* Smuggling carries genre momentum: pursuit, deadline, cargo,
   violence, money. The prose this collection has built is not built to run. The fix is a
   cargo not worth anything dramatic and a run that is mostly waiting — the tide, the pass,
   the fog, the cutter that never comes.

## 4. Ledger position

No collision. Haunt logics used: *something is out there with the children* (01), *a figure
nobody can date and one man unaffected* (03). Ending types used: recontextualization, door
left open — **continuance is unspent**, though L-006 is n=1 and has fired twelve times and
is the most over-trusted rule in the repo, so a continuance ending here needs an argument
rather than a default. Physical band: explicit (01), edge (03) — a threatened story is the
one the book is missing, which cuts against taking `the-encrusted-man` for this.
