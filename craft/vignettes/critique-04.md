# Critique 04 — Vignette 1, revision 04 (the ending)

## Mark, 2026-09-08

> "I really like the end as well. It was an inconvenience for her to use the kitchen
> door but she did it to avoid going by the stairs, and this was a good way of
> describing it. I want to make sure we understand this mechanism and capture it

---

## Was it a fluke?

Partly. The repo happens to contain the control.

**Draft 01** ended: *"...she went around by the pantry, which added eleven steps."*
Same detour, same number, and it was inert — I listed it in my own handover note as an
unremarkable behavioural close.

**Revision 04** ends: *"She went back in by the kitchen door. She used the kitchen door
for the rest of the summer, which added eleven steps each way."*

Three things changed, and the difference between them is the mechanism:

1. **There is now fear in front of it.** Draft 01 priced a fear the reader had never
   felt. A cost is only information if the reader knows what is being bought.
2. **It was promoted to its own sentence, in final position.** In draft 01 the number
   was a subordinate clause inside a longer sentence about something else.
3. **It gained a duration** — "for the rest of the summer" — which converts a single
   reaction into a standing arrangement.

So: the *detail* was luck, and it sat in the draft doing nothing for two rounds. The
*placement and the duration* were deliberate. What neither of us had was the reason it
works, which is below and is now L-006.

---

## The mechanism, taken apart

**1. The price is quantified; the fear never is.** Eleven steps is countable, trivial,
and exact. The thing on the landing is not described, not named, and not referred to
again. The reader divides one by the other and arrives at the size of the fear on
their own. *You measure a feeling by what someone will pay to avoid it.*

**2. The price is small, and that is why it works.** If she had left the house, the
reaction would be proportionate to a fright and would tell us nothing new. Eleven
steps is an *accommodation* — she has not fled it or fought it, she has built a small
permanent workaround and gone on with her life. That implies the thing is a settled
condition of the house and that she knows it. She has made an arrangement.

**3. The cause is never joined to the cost.** The word *stairs* does not appear in the
last paragraph. The reader closes that gap themselves, in one step, and the closing is
what makes them complicit.

**4. Interiority is withdrawn.** The final two sentences have no inside of her head at
all — after her legs go on the step, the narration retreats to pure logistics. The
withdrawal *is* the effect: she has stopped having a crisis about it, because it is
just her life now.

**5. Duration replaces escalation.** Nothing gets worse. It gets permanent. That is the
book's ending type (`craft-principles.md` §7) and this is the executable version of it.

## Boundary note — why this is not L-003 bookkeeping

The reader has to infer *kitchen door → avoiding the stairs*, which looks like the
clerical work L-003/004/005 ban. It is not, and the distinction matters:

**Does the reader complete the inference, or search for it?**

Here every piece is present, the step is single, the answer is unambiguous, and
arriving at it is a small pleasure. Bookkeeping is re-parsing words already held,
hunting for material that was never supplied, or holding a contradiction open. The
good gap is *closed*; the bad gap is *searched*.
