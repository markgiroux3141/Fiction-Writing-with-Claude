# Vignette Batch 01 — Revision 03

Vignette 1, rewritten against critique 02 (L-001 ceiling, L-002, L-003, L-004).

---

## 1 — The staircase  *(A1: presence with nothing shown; no impossible thing)*

She was halfway across the hall with the mending basket when she knew there was
somebody at the top of the stairs, looking down at her.

She did not look. She kept going for the door at the same pace, because to go faster
was to admit it, and the hall was nine feet and took a long time.

Her back had never been so much of her before. She could feel the whole width of it.

The basket was on the wrong hip. It was not on the wrong hip, but that was the thought
she had, and she carried it to the door and worked the latch twice before it went up.

Outside it was ten in the morning and white hot. She set the basket on the step for
the Boudreaux girl, and then her legs went, and she sat down beside it in full view of
the road with her head down until it passed.

She went back in by the kitchen door. She used the kitchen door for the rest of the
summer, which added eleven steps each way.

---

## What changed

**Fear made stupid instead of observant.** The r2 draft gave her an anatomist's
attention at the worst possible moment. She now notices almost nothing, and what she
does notice is wrong: the basket is on the wrong hip, and it isn't.

**The neck is gone; the state is not.** *"Her back had never been so much of her
before. She could feel the whole width of it."* Two flat sentences, no mechanism —
the experience the idiom was pointing at (L-002).

**The body arrives late.** Nothing happens to her in the hall except that she keeps
walking. The legs go on the step, outside, in the sun, after it is over, in front of
the whole road — which is both true to how fright actually lands and a better measure
of its size than anything she could have felt at the time.

**Cuts.** "It was wet there" — gone, no plain referent to decode (L-003). "A fault in
the building and not in her" — gone, along with the whole architectural preamble; the
paragraph now starts in the middle of the crossing (L-004). The thermometer went with
it; the heat is now "ten in the morning and white hot," which needs no instrument.

**~175 words**, back inside the cap. The r2 overrun was the over-rendering, not the
subject.

## Carried forward

Vignettes **3 (The handover)** and **6 (The worn chair)** in `batch-01.md` both use
the same witty-aside move that L-004 now bans — *"which was nothing, except that"* and
*"nothing on the list he had not written a hundred times."* Neither sits at the
threshold of a fright, so they may survive; flagging rather than pre-emptively fixing,
since 2–6 have not been scored yet.
