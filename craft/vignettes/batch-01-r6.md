# Vignette Batch 01 — Revision 06

Vignettes 2–5, against critique 06 (L-008 … L-011). **1 stands at r4; 6 stands at r5**
(uncritiqued).

Each now carries a **Rule (off-page)** — one sentence saying what the thing wants and
what it does. It never appears in the prose. This is L-008 and it is the answer to
"what's happening in this story exactly?"

---

## 2 — The extra setting  *(B1: dread as arithmetic and decision)*

> **Rule (off-page):** Something has eaten at that table for years. Eight settings
> always went out; with eight boarders in the house, nobody could see it. The Pardue
> boy leaving dropped the count to seven and left it standing in the open.

There were seven at Mrs. Ivey's that spring, and she had laid for seven since the
Pardue boy went home in February, and her hands did it without her. So she was most of
the way back to the kitchen with the empty tray before she understood what she had
walked past.

Eight places. The eighth one down at the foot end — fork, knife, spoon, the plate
squared to the table edge, the napkin folded the way she folded them and nobody else in
the parish did.

The men would be down in four minutes. She stood there for two of them.

To take it up meant she had set it. To leave it meant she had not. She left it. Nobody
sat in it, and nobody said a word about it, and when she came to clear, the plate had
been eaten off. Not much. The way a person eats who is being careful in company.

She laid eight the next night and every night after. She washed that plate herself, in
her own water, once the girl had gone up.

## 3 — The handover  *(D1: touch; the impossible as courtesy)*

> **Rule (off-page):** Something in that pond gives things back. It has been doing it a
> long time, it is not particular about what, and what it hands you does not come back
> right.

He lost the knife off the gunwale in three feet of water. The bottom was silt and
it would be gone in a minute, so he went over the side.

The water came to his chest and was the temperature of a bath somebody had got out of
an hour before. Nothing moved away from him when he came in. A pond that size, you put
a leg in and the whole of it goes somewhere else. Nothing went anywhere.

He put his arm down and swept the bottom in the flat way you do, wrist loose, palm
up. He found the handle. He had it.

And then it was pressed into his hand, closed, and the fingers folded over his own to
be sure of it, the way you would give a man his change.

He came out of the water in one go, over the side and into the boat, and sat with his
knees drawn up and the knife in his fist.

It never took an edge again. He kept it and went on stropping it.

He lost a pair of pliers off that same gunwale in September and a whetstone the year
after. He let them both go.

## 4 — The game  *(I1: tonal inversion; daylight)*

> **Rule (off-page):** It learns a game by watching and then plays it correctly. It
> will keep playing after the children have stopped, and it will come in if it is
> called.

It was the fourth day of the heat and the children had got into the linen press, which
she would deal with later. From the gallery she could see them going around the fig
tree. Two under sheets, three not. The ones under sheets went slow with their arms out,
because that was the rule.

She shelled peas. After a while a third sheet came around the far side of the tree.

It was taller. It went the same slow way, arms out, keeping to the rule, and the
children screamed at it properly. It followed the Ledoux girl twice around and let her
get away, which was correct. She thought Hollis had come up from the field to play.
Hollis was not a man who played.

She called them in at noon. Four came. Then the fifth, pulling the sheet off her head
as she came up the steps. She counted them at the table and got five, and counted again
and got five.

The sixth stayed out by the fig tree with its arms down at its sides, in the full sun.

She kept the press locked after that and gave the sheets out herself.

## 5 — Trimming  *(G1: explicit vegetal physical, flat delivery)*

> **Rule (off-page):** He is being grown into. It came up through him years ago, it
> puts out more in the wet, and it can be cut but not pulled. He has decided to keep
> the appointment rather than lose the face.

Sunday he trimmed his beard at the window, where the light was better, with the glass
propped against the sash and a square of newspaper across his knees.

It came in faster in the wet months. He took the outside first — the long threads that
had got down past the second button and gone pale at the ends, the way a thing does when
it has run out of something. Those cut easy. Closer in it was tougher and had a grain to
it, so you went across rather than straight on, and it was wet at the root end and came
off on his fingers and smelled of the underside of a boat.

He worked up under the jaw by feel. Some of it went through the skin and some of it only
lay against the skin, and after eleven years he could tell the difference in the dark.
He did not pull on the ones that went through. He had done that once.

He folded the newspaper over and put it in the stove. He had the window up before he did
it, on account of the smell.

# What changed, and why

## #2 — the hollow filled

The rule decides everything the r5 draft was vague about, and it changes the ending
from a gesture into a mechanism:

- **Why now.** The count dropped from eight to seven in February. The thing was always
  there; the arithmetic hid it.
- **Why her napkin fold.** It has watched her lay that table for years.
- **Who sits in it.** Nobody sits in it. Mr. Dell is gone from the draft — in r5 he sat
  in the eighth place, which muddled the question badly.
- **Why it is frightening.** *The plate had been eaten off.* That is the new line, and
  it converts the accommodation ending: she does not wash it to hide that she set it.
  She washes it because it is dirty, and she does it in her own water after the girl has
  gone up. She is feeding it nightly and concealing the evidence, and nothing in the
  paragraph says so.

*"Not much. The way a person eats who is being careful in company"* is a simile that
classifies a behaviour, permitted under the L-007 boundary.

## #3 — approach, consequence, and a reason for the ending

- **Wrongness before contact (L-010).** *"Nothing moved away from him when he came in.
  A pond that size, you put a leg in it and the whole of it goes somewhere else;
  nothing went anywhere."* An absence-signal: an ordinary thing failing to happen. It
  costs nothing and does not spend the one impossible thing.
- **Consequence (L-008).** *"It never took an edge again. He kept it and went on
  stropping it."* Something is measurably different afterward, and the futility of
  stropping a knife that will not take an edge is the whole of it.
- **The ending now has a reason the reader can reach.** Pliers and a whetstone, let
  go. Given the ruined knife, the inference is available in one step: *you do not take
  back what it hands you.* Never stated. The whetstone is deliberate — he is buying
  the tool for the job the knife will no longer do.

## #4 — construction varied, technique kept (L-011)

The chains are broken up by other means, not eliminated. Counting ", and" joins in the
prose: r5 had five, r6 has two — *"and the children screamed at it properly"*, kept
because the run-on carries the children's excitement, and *"and counted again and got
five"*, which is repetition that intensifies rather than extends (L-005) and is the
arithmetic lever doing its work.

New full stops where an *and* was: *"Hollis was not a man who played."* now lands as
its own sentence instead of trailing off a chain. *"Four came. Then the fifth…"* stages
the count in beats rather than in a run, which is the lever. And **"The sixth stayed
out by the fig tree"** is now its own paragraph — in r5 it was the tail of a chain,
which was the single worst placement decision in the batch.

## #5 — the frame, said out loud (L-009)

**"Sunday he trimmed his beard at the window."** Six words, and the paragraph is no
longer a puzzle. Nothing was spoiled: the horror was never *what is he doing*, it was
what the beard turns out to be, and that is now free to arrive across the following
three paragraphs instead of competing with a comprehension problem.

The enamel bowl is gone — an ambiguous prop pulling toward fish — replaced by **the
glass propped against the sash**, which can only mean a man looking at his own face.

Also changed: *"because of what pulling on them did"* → **"He had done that once."**
Flatter, concrete, and it implies an incident without describing one.

## Standing

#6 (The worn chair) is unrevised and now the only vignette without a stated rule.
Written honestly, mine would be: *she is still in the chair.* That is thin, and it is
exactly the kind of thin L-008 is meant to catch — worth deciding properly before that
lever gets used in a story.
