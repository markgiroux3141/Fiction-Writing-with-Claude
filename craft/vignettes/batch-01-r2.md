## 1 — The staircase  *(A1: presence with nothing shown; no impossible thing)*

The stairs came down into the hall at an angle that put the top of them behind you
before you reached the front door, which was a fault in the building and not in her.
She had gone through twice that morning and thought nothing. The third time she was
carrying the mending basket on her hip, and halfway across, the hair on the back of
her neck came away from her skin.

It was wet there. It had been wet there since six. So it did not rise all at once. It
unstuck, from the nape up, a few and then a few more, and she felt every one of them
let go.

Then the arms, from the shoulder down, in a hall where the thermometer by the door
read eighty-eight.

Then the knowing: someone stood on the landing behind her, hands at their sides,
looking down at the exact piece of her that had told her so.

She did not turn. She got her forearm up over the back of her neck and held it there,
which did no good, and she held it there anyway until she was out on the step in the
sun.

After that she took the pantry way around, which added eleven steps, and did it that
way the rest of the summer.

---

## What changed, and why

**The order is inverted.** Draft 01 ran *knowing → behaviour*. It now runs **body →
knowing → behaviour**, which is L-001 and the new `style-guide.md` § Emotion. She does
not become frightened and then feel her neck; her neck is how she finds out.

**The hair is wet.** Dry hair standing up is the stock image and compresses to four
words. Sweat-damp hair has to *unstick*, which gives the event duration — it happens
over three clauses, a few hairs at a time, which is the pace at which the reader's own
neck has a chance to answer. Gulf heat was already in the setting; it is now the
instrument.

**The gooseflesh contradicts the weather.** Eighty-eight degrees on the hall
thermometer. Cold as a supernatural signal is banned (`ai-tells.md` §A); the body
going cold *against* the conditions is a fact, and it also gives her a fussiness —
she is a woman who reads the thermometer and counts steps.

**The neck is the object.** "Looking down at the exact piece of her that had told her
so" — the part that reacted is the part being looked at. The reader has been carrying
that spot around since the first sentence, which says the top of the stairs is behind
*you*.

**The defence is useless and she keeps it up.** The forearm over the nape replaces the
old line "she had the sense that looking would be a kind of answer" — cut because it
was a thought where the paragraph now wants a body. Say if you want it back; it was
the best sentence in draft 01 and it is a real loss.

**Length:** ~210 words, over the bank's 200 cap. Physiology at this resolution costs
words. If the trade is right, the cap moves.
