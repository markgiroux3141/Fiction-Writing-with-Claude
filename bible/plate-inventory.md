# Plate Inventory

The five wet-plate photographs and the cover. Each plate is a story seed; the
description here is what a reader sees, and the "openings" are angles that have not
yet been written.

Files currently carry generator filenames. Proposed slugs are listed for renaming
once confirmed.

## Cover — *Standing Water: Of Roots, Reflections, and the Faces Beneath*
`cover art/cover.png`

Distressed black cloth board, gilt lettering, a mounted albumen print of Plate III
set into the front board. Establishes the book as an artifact — a found album rather
than an anthology. Worth deciding early whether that framing is literal (see

## Plate I — The Sheet
`wet plate photographs/ChatGPT Image Sep 4, 2026, 12_16_59 PM.png`
→ proposed: `plate-01-the-sheet.png`

A figure under a white sheet stands on the landing of a ruined staircase. Two
cut eyeholes, black and perfectly round. Peeling plaster, cobwebbed banister,
water-stained wall. The costume is a child's idea of a ghost, worn by something the
size of an adult, and the drape falls as though over a real body.

**What's frightening:** the deliberateness. Someone *put this on*. The sheet is a
disguise, which means there is a reason to be disguised.

**Openings:** a mourning household's photographic hoax that produced a real
exposure; a game played by children in a house where an adult joined in; the sheet
as a shroud repurposed, and what it was taken from.

## Plate II — The Climb
`wet plate photographs/ChatGPT Image Sep 4, 2026, 12_20_46 PM.png`
→ proposed: `plate-02-the-climb.png`

A root-mass in the rough shape of a long-limbed animal ascends the same ruined
staircase. Roots have grown up through the floorboards, sheathed the newel post, and
gathered themselves into something with a spine and forelimbs. It is caught
mid-motion, going up.

**What's frightening:** direction. It is climbing *toward* the bedrooms. And the
house is feeding it.

**Openings:** a house abandoned but not empty; the thing under the floor that has
been drinking the same water for eighty years; roots as the physical form of
inheritance.

## Plate III — Standing Water *(title plate)*
`wet plate photographs/ChatGPT Image Sep 6, 2026, 03_47_06 PM.png`
→ proposed: `plate-03-standing-water.png`

A veiled woman in a pale nineteenth-century dress stands at the edge of a flooded
wood. Cypress, saw grass, flat brown water. Her face behind the veil is a blank.
Below her, the reflection — and the reflection is not quite hers. It hangs lower,
longer, and the water shows a shape the bank does not.

**What's frightening:** the reflection is the true body; the figure on the bank is
what it is projecting.

**Openings:** the title story. A drowning that the water declined to finish; a
family that has been marrying the same debt for generations; someone who comes to
photograph the site and gets an extra figure in the plate.

**Status:** assigned to `stories/01-standing-water/`.

## Plate IV — The Attitude of Grief
`wet plate photographs/ChatGPT Image Sep 6, 2026, 03_50_31 PM.png`
→ proposed: `plate-04-attitude-of-grief.png`

A winged figure stands among overgrown graves — Celtic cross, ivy, a
flower-carved headstone. Wings fully spread. But the face is not carved: it is
slack, exhausted, human, hair hanging in damp ropes. Bare feet on the leaf litter.
The stone drapery has the give of wet cloth.

**What's frightening:** it is not a statue that came alive. It is a woman who has
been standing there long enough to be mistaken for one.

**Openings:** a memorial commissioned from a living model who never left; the
cemetery's angel that appears in a different plot each time it is photographed;
grief as a posture you can be caught in permanently.

## Plate V — The Sitter
`wet plate photographs/ChatGPT Image Sep 6, 2026, 04_00_33 PM.png`
→ proposed: `plate-05-the-sitter.png`

Studio portrait, formal dress — frock coat, cravat, pinned collar. The sitter's
head is wrong: hairless mottled scalp, enormous black lensless eyes, a beak-like
nose, and a beard that is not hair but fine root-fibre, still growing, hanging in
threads past the collar. Hands not visible. The pose is correct and patient.

**What's frightening:** the composure. It sat for the full exposure. It wanted a
portrait.

**Openings:** a photographer's ledger with one sitting unaccounted for; the thing
that arrives dressed as a gentleman and pays in coin that is too old; what the roots
in Plate II grow into if left long enough. The strongest candidate in the set for a
genuinely explicit story — the face is already doing the work.

## Cross-plate vocabulary

Recurring elements to deploy deliberately, not accidentally: **roots** (I, II, V),
**standing/flat water** (III), **veils and coverings** (I, III), **the staircase**
(I, II — same house), **wrong faces** (III, IV, V), **patience** (IV, V).

Plates I and II are demonstrably the same staircase — but the collection is
standalone, so this is a visual rhyme only, not a shared setting. If both plates get
stories, the two houses are different houses that happen to be dying the same way.
